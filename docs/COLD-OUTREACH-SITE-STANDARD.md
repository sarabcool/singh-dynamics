# Cold Outreach Site Standard

This document defines the default site scope for prospect previews used in cold outreach.

## Default rule

For any real cold-call or cold-email prospect that Sarab does not already know, build a **multi-page website preview**, not a one-page landing page.

The preview should feel like a site the business could realistically keep and launch with minimal restructuring. A cold prospect should not receive something that feels like a quick mockup, template demo, or single-page agency pitch.

## Minimum structure

Unless the business genuinely does not support the content, default to at least:

- Home
- Services
- About
- Contact

Add additional pages when the verified business information supports them, such as:

- Individual service pages
- FAQ
- Gallery or work examples
- Locations or service areas
- Industries served
- Financing, warranty, or process pages only when those claims are verified

## Quality bar

Each page should have a real purpose and enough content to stand on its own. Do not split a one-page site into several thin pages just to satisfy the page count.

The site should:

- feel custom to the prospect's business and trade
- use verified public information only
- avoid fabricated reviews, hours, addresses, claims, warranties, turnaround times, years in business, pricing, or credentials
- preserve `noindex,nofollow` on private prospect previews
- leave the prospect's production domain and DNS untouched until explicitly authorized
- include clear navigation between pages
- work properly on mobile
- use strong local-business calls to action without pretending the prospect has approved the site

## Exceptions

A one-page preview is still acceptable when:

- the lead is warm, internal, a friend/family business, or already known to Sarab
- Sarab explicitly requests a one-page concept
- there is not enough verified information to support multiple useful pages
- the preview is only a quick design experiment rather than actual cold outreach

When in doubt for an unknown prospect, choose **multi-page**.

## Reason

Cold outreach has one chance to establish credibility. A fuller site makes the preview feel like a real deliverable rather than a vibe-coded mockup, gives the prospect more to evaluate, and better demonstrates what Singh Dynamics would actually build if they become a client.

This rule applies to future prospect-site generation, outreach preparation, and site-design decisions unless Sarab explicitly overrides it for a specific lead.
