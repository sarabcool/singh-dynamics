How it works

01

Connect your books and your mailbox

QuickBooks Online is the source of truth for invoices, balances, and due dates. Follow-up goes out from your own mailbox, in your own thread.

02

It watches, follows up, and reads replies

Overdue invoices get routine follow-up automatically. Customer replies get read and understood, and payment promises get recorded.

03

A human decides the exceptions

Disputes, term changes, discounts, and anything outside your configured authority get queued for you. Nothing outside policy sends on its own.

The rule behind it

Automate the administrative work. Gate changes to business terms.

The model can classify, summarize, draft, and propose. A deterministic policy engine decides whether a proposed action is allowed, requires your approval, or is blocked outright. The model never grants itself authority it wasn't given.

Scope, stated plainly

V1 is commercial B2B invoice follow-up software, not consumer debt collection. It does not purchase debt, threaten legal action, report to credit bureaus, invent fees, or waive balances outside owner-defined authority. Singh AR is in active development.
