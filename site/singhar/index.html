Get paid without chasing.

In active development

It watches what is open, and follows up from your own mailbox.

Singh AR connects to QuickBooks Online and your business mailbox. It sends routine follow-up inside the existing thread, reads what the customer writes back, and stops the moment something changes what is actually owed.

One overdue invoice, end to end
Runs on its own Waits for a person

Automate routine. Gate exceptions.

The model classifies, summarises and drafts. It never decides whether it is allowed to act. A separate deterministic policy engine makes that call, which means the boundary of what this software can do to a customer relationship is something you set and can read, not something you hope was inferred correctly at three in the morning.

Where the line sits.

Does on its own

  • Watches open invoices and due dates in QuickBooks Online
  • Sends routine follow-up from your mailbox, in the existing thread
  • Reads replies and understands what the customer actually said
  • Records a promise to pay and follows up when that date passes
  • Stops chasing the moment an invoice is settled

Brings to you

  • A customer disputing the amount or the work
  • Any request for a discount or a write-off
  • Changes to payment terms
  • Anything the policy engine cannot place inside your configured authority
  • Replies that do not clearly parse as one of the known cases

What it will never do.

  • Purchase debt or act as a collections agency.
  • Threaten legal action.
  • Report anyone to a credit bureau.
  • Invent fees or waive balances outside your authority.
  • Collect consumer debt.

Commercial B2B invoicing software. Not a collections product.

Status

In active development. Not generally available.

The deterministic core and the policy model are being built first, deliberately, because they are what make the rest safe to run unattended. Nothing on this page describes a feature you can buy today.
