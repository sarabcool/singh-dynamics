Automate the routine. Gate the exceptions.

The model can classify, summarise, draft and propose. It never decides whether it is allowed to act. A separate deterministic policy engine makes that call, which means the boundary of what this software can do to a customer relationship is something you set and can read, not something you hope the model inferred correctly at 3am.

Where the line sits

Does on its own

  • Watches open invoices and due dates in QuickBooks Online
  • Sends routine follow-up from your mailbox, in the existing thread
  • Reads replies and understands what the customer actually said
  • Records a promise to pay and follows up when that date passes
  • Stops chasing the moment an invoice is settled

Brings to you

  • A customer disputing the amount or the work
  • Any request for a discount or a write-off
  • Changes to payment terms
  • Anything the policy engine cannot place inside your configured authority
  • Replies that do not clearly parse as one of the known cases

Will not do

  • Purchase debt or act as a collections agency
  • Threaten legal action
  • Report anyone to a credit bureau
  • Invent fees or waive balances outside your authority
  • Consumer debt collection. This is commercial B2B software
